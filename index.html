Gainline

Gym tracking for iPhone and Apple Watch: weights, gym cardio, live heart rate, progress stats and calories.

Support

Found a bug or have an idea? Email baileywilson12@icloud.com. You can also send feedback in the app from Profile → Settings → Send Feedback.

Common questions

Where is my data? Your workouts are on your iPhone, your Apple Watch and Apple Health, and backed up to your private iCloud (a workout's heart rate and calories aren't in that backup; they stay on your iPhone and in Apple Health). Workouts you share in the League are stored in Gainline's League database (Apple's CloudKit) so your followers can see them, with the calories and heart rate you choose to share. See the Privacy Policy for everything.

Heart rate strap. Pair a Bluetooth heart rate strap in Gainline → Profile → Settings → Connections → Heart Rate Sensors & Watches.

Heart rate isn't showing. Make sure Gainline has Heart Rate access in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Gainline, and that your watch band is snug.

Cancel a subscription. Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Gainline.
