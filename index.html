Gainline

Gym tracking for iPhone and Apple Watch: weights, gym cardio, live heart rate, progress stats and calories.

Support

Found a bug or have an idea? Email baileywilson12@icloud.com. If you're testing the beta, you can also send feedback from the TestFlight app, or take a screenshot in Gainline and TestFlight will offer to send it.

Common questions

Where is my data? On your iPhone, your Apple Watch, Apple Health and your private iCloud. Deleting and reinstalling Gainline keeps it.

Heart rate isn't showing. Make sure Gainline has Heart Rate access in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Gainline, and that your watch band is snug.

Cancel a subscription. Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Gainline.
