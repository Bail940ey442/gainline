Gainline Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 November 2026. See also our Terms of Use.

Health and workout data stays private unless you share it

With your permission, Gainline reads from Apple Health: workouts and their routes, heart rate, active and resting calories, food calories, VO₂ max, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, heart rate recovery, walking heart rate, respiratory rate, blood oxygen, sleep, steps, flights climbed, exercise minutes, distance, swimming strokes, workout effort, height, weight, body fat, lean body mass, date of birth and sex. It uses them on your iPhone for your progress, calories, heart rate zones, trends and comparisons with people of your age and sex. It writes to Apple Health the workouts you record (with heart rate, calories, distance, swimming strokes, steps, effort and the GPS route of outdoor workouts), food calories, and measurements you enter yourself. Gainline never writes estimated numbers to Apple Health.

Heart rate and calories stay out of your iCloud backup. Your workouts, sets, routines, food log, the body measurements you enter yourself and your settings are stored on your iPhone (encrypted while it's locked), shared with your own Apple Watch, and backed up to your private iCloud account so they survive reinstalling the app or moving to a new phone. A workout's heart rate and calories, which come from Apple Health or your heart rate sensor, are kept on your iPhone and in Apple Health and are not included in that iCloud backup; on a new phone Gainline reads them again from Apple Health. Data in your private iCloud is only accessible to you; we can't see it. Your workouts only leave your devices when you choose to share them in the League (below), and heart rate and calories are only included there if your League & Sharing switches allow it. Gainline never uses your data for advertising or marketing and never sells it.

Location during outdoor workouts

When you start an outdoor workout or a field sport, Gainline uses your iPhone's GPS for as long as the workout runs, including while the screen is locked or you're in another app (the blue location indicator shows while it does), so your route, distance and pace are recorded. It stops when the workout ends. The route is saved with the workout on your iPhone and in Apple Health. It is never shared in the League or sent to our server, and Gainline doesn't use your location at any other time.

Bluetooth heart rate sensors

If you pair a Bluetooth heart rate strap or armband in Profile → Settings → Connections, Gainline receives your heart rate from it during workouts, including while the screen is locked or you're in another app, so your heart rate keeps recording. It's treated exactly like heart rate from Apple Health: kept on your iPhone, saved to Apple Health with the workout, and only shared in the League if you switch on Share Heart Rate.

The League

You create a League profile when you set up Gainline. What you choose to share is stored in Gainline's public CloudKit database (part of Apple's iCloud) so other Gainline users can see it: your username, name, bio and profile photo; the workouts you share (with their stats, sets, calories, any selfie, the name of the gym if you tag one, and a heart rate summary if you share heart rate); your weekly totals and streak, and your best weight on each lift (so friends can compare lifts with you); competitions, comments, kudos and reactions; who you follow; and, if Go Live is on, your workout as it happens (time, sets, distance and speed, calories and heart rate if you share them, and the song you're playing if you share it). Heart rate from Apple Health is only shared if you switch on Share Heart Rate, when you create your profile or later; calories are shared unless you switch Share Calories off. If you tag your gym, its name is shown on the workouts you share, as content you choose to add; its map location is never shared, and neither is your own location or route. You control this in Profile → Settings (gear) → League & Sharing. In Privacy & Safety you can make your account followers-only, approve new followers, hide yourself from search, hide who you follow, and choose who can comment and send you competitions. In What Followers See you choose which stats are shared (heart rate, calories, sets, photos, gym and more). You can also stop sharing at any time. If you block someone, your profile stores a one-way code of them (not who they are) so their app hides you from them. Deleting your League profile removes it.

What the League shares. If you create a League profile, the workouts you record, your live workouts and your stories are shared automatically with the people who can see your profile, except workouts you mark Personal. Turn on Keep My Workouts Private in Profile → Settings → Privacy & Safety to stop sharing them with anyone. The switches in League & Sharing choose which details are included (heart rate, calories, weights, photos and so on).

Treat what a public League account shares as public. The League's database can be read by anyone using Gainline, so for an account that isn't followers-only, someone with technical skills could see what you've shared even where the app doesn't show it to them. Only share what you're comfortable being public. Your private workouts, any health numbers you haven't chosen to share and anything else you haven't shared are never in it.

Followers-only accounts are encrypted. If you turn on Only Followers, the workouts you share (their details, stats and photos), your weekly totals, your live workouts and the text of comments on them are encrypted on your iPhone before they are stored, and the key is given only to the followers you approve. We can't read them and neither can other Gainline users. The key is kept in your iCloud Keychain and your private iCloud database. What this does not hide: your name, username, bio and profile photo; who follows whom; that you posted or are training right now, and when (but not what); who commented or gave kudos on something; the size of what you shared; and competitions, which stay readable so the people you compete with can see them. Someone you remove or block can't read anything you share afterwards, but keeps whatever their phone had already downloaded. Anything you shared while your account was public may already have been seen. A new follower can read your workouts once your iPhone has next opened Gainline, and followers need a current version of Gainline. Only Followers always turns on Approve Follow Requests, so you choose who is given the key.

Safety and age

The League is for people 13 and over. Reports of posts, comments and profiles are reviewed within 24 hours, and content that breaks the Community Guidelines is removed. Comments, follows and competitions are rate-limited to stop spam.

Your rights

You can download a copy of your workouts and League profile any time in Profile → Settings → League & Sharing → Privacy & Safety → Download My Data, delete your League profile, erase your workouts, or delete your account in Profile → Settings. For anything else, email us below.

Finding friends from your contacts (optional)

If you allow access to your contacts, Gainline turns each contact's phone number and email into a scrambled one-way code on your iPhone and sends only those codes to Gainline's server, which checks them against the League and replies with which of your contacts are on Gainline. The codes aren't kept, and names, numbers and emails never leave your iPhone. If you choose to let friends find you, a one-way code of the phone number or email you enter is stored on Gainline's server (Supabase, in London) and linked to your League profile, never the number or email itself. The code is keyed again with a secret only the server has, and the server only tells other users whether a code they already have matches someone, with limits on how often they can ask. Deleting your account deletes it. You can add them when you set up Gainline, and change or clear them any time in Settings.

Usage statistics

Gainline counts which screens and features are used, as anonymous daily totals, to improve the app. To see where people get stuck in sign-up and on the subscription screen, Gainline also sends anonymous events (for example "reached question 3" or "started a free trial", plus the app version, iOS version and language) to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service based in the EU. The daily totals go there too. Both use a one-way code made from a random install ID, never your name, email, Apple ID, contacts or health data, and can't be linked to you. Turn both off in Profile → Settings (gear) → Privacy & Data → Share Anonymous Usage.

Meal estimates with Claude (optional)

If you add your own Anthropic API key, meals you describe in Log Food are sent directly from your iPhone to Anthropic so Claude can estimate their calories. Only the text you type is sent, never your health data, and it doesn't pass through our servers. Your key is kept in your iPhone's Keychain and usage is billed to your Anthropic account. Anthropic's privacy policy covers data sent to them. Remove the key any time in Profile → Settings (gear) → Connections → Claude.

Feedback

If you send feedback from Profile → Settings → Send Feedback, we receive your message, the type (bug or idea), your app version, iPhone model and iOS version, and a screenshot if you add one. It's stored on our server with your random account identifier so we can follow up in the app, and only we can read it. Ask us to delete it any time.

Our server

Gainline's server runs on Supabase in London, UK. It holds only what's described under Sign in with Apple, Referrals and creator codes, Subscriptions and Feedback. Your workouts and health data are never sent to it.

Sign in with Apple (optional)

You can sign in to Gainline with Apple. Your name and email stay on your iPhone. Our server only receives a one-way hash of your Apple user ID, to keep your referral code with your account. Deleting your account in Profile → Settings removes this and revokes Gainline's access to your Apple ID.

Referrals and creator codes

Each install of Gainline gets a random account identifier on our server (not your name, email or Apple ID). If you use referrals or redeem a creator's code (creator codes are Apple offer codes, redeemed through Apple's own Redeem a Code screen), the server stores your code, the referral or offer code used, and the App Store transaction details needed to confirm a subscription (product, price, dates, refunds), apply free months, and pay creators their share. Creators only ever see totals, never who subscribed. You can delete this in Profile → Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete My Referral Data, or by deleting your account.

Subscriptions

Payments are handled by Apple. We don't see your payment details. Apple tells our server about purchases, renewals and refunds of Gainline subscriptions, so free months and creator shares are correct.

Who processes your data, and for how long

Apple (iCloud and CloudKit), Supabase (our server) and TelemetryDeck (anonymous usage statistics) process data only on our behalf and for the purposes above, and provide the same or equal protection of your data as this policy. Anthropic only receives meal text if you add your own key, under Anthropic's privacy policy. We keep data only as long as needed: your private iCloud data and League posts until you delete them or your account; friend-finding codes are checked and not kept; feedback until it's dealt with, and no longer than 12 months; referral and subscription records for as long as you have an account (and as tax law requires for creator payments); anonymous usage statistics under TelemetryDeck's retention policy, and since they can't be linked to you they can't be traced back to your account. Deleting your account removes everything we hold on our server.

No tracking

Gainline has no ads, never sells data and doesn't track you across apps or websites.
