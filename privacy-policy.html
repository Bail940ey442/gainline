Gainline Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 November 2026.

Health and workout data stays on your devices

Gainline reads and writes workouts, heart rate, calories, swimming distance and strokes, VO₂ max, resting heart rate, heart rate variability and heart rate recovery through Apple Health, only with your permission. Your workouts, sets, routines, food log and settings are stored on your iPhone (encrypted while it's locked), shared with your own Apple Watch, and backed up to your private iCloud account so they survive reinstalling the app or moving to a new phone. Data in your private iCloud is only accessible to you; we can't see it. Your workouts only leave your devices when you choose to share them in the League (below) or send them to Strava. Gainline never uses your data for advertising and never sells it.

The League (optional)

If you create a League profile, what you choose to share is stored in Gainline's CloudKit database so the people you allow can see it: your username, name, bio and profile photo; the workouts you share (with their stats, sets, heart rate summary and any selfie); your weekly totals and streak; competitions, comments, kudos and reactions; who you follow; and, if Go Live is on, your workout as it happens (time, heart rate, calories, sets, distance and speed, and the song you're playing if you share it). Your location and route are never shared. You control this in Settings → Privacy & Safety: make your account followers-only, approve new followers, hide yourself from search, hide who you follow, choose who can comment and send you competitions, choose which stats are shared, or stop sharing. If you block someone, your profile stores a one-way code of them (not who they are) so their app hides you from them. Deleting your League profile removes it.

Safety and age

The League is for people 13 and over. Reports of posts, comments and profiles are reviewed within 24 hours, and content that breaks the Community Guidelines is removed. Comments, follows and competitions are rate-limited to stop spam.

Your rights

You can download a copy of your workouts and League profile any time in Settings → Privacy & Safety → Download My Data, delete your League profile, erase your workouts, or delete your account in Settings. For anything else, email us below.

Finding friends from your contacts (optional)

If you allow access to your contacts, Gainline turns each contact's phone number and email into a one-way code on your iPhone and checks only those codes against the League, to show which of your contacts are on Gainline. Your contacts are never uploaded. If you choose to let friends find you, a one-way code of the phone number or email you enter is stored with your profile, never the number or email itself. Clear them any time in Find People → Let Friends Find You.

Usage statistics

Gainline counts which screens and features are used, as anonymous daily totals with a random install ID that isn't linked to you, to improve the app. Turn this off in Settings → Privacy & Data.

Meal estimates with Claude (optional)

If you add your own Anthropic API key, meals you describe in Log Food are sent directly from your iPhone to Anthropic so Claude can estimate their calories. Only the text you type is sent, never your health data, and it doesn't pass through our servers. Your key is kept in your iPhone's Keychain and usage is billed to your Anthropic account. Anthropic's privacy policy covers data sent to them. Remove the key any time in Profile → Integrations → Claude.

Strava (optional)

If you connect Strava, Gainline sends the workouts you choose (or, if you turn it on, each new workout) to your Strava account: title, time, duration, heart rate, calories, distance, and your sets, laps or steps. This only happens after you connect and agree. Data on Strava is covered by Strava's privacy policy. Your Strava sign-in tokens are kept in your iPhone's Keychain; our server only exchanges them with Strava and doesn't store them. Disconnect any time in Profile.

Sign in with Apple (optional)

Your name and email stay on your iPhone. Our server only receives a one-way hash of your Apple user ID, to keep your referral code with your account. Deleting your account in Profile removes this and revokes Gainline's access to your Apple ID.

Referrals

If you use referrals, our server stores a random account identifier from the App Store (not your name, email or Apple ID), your referral code, and the App Store transaction IDs needed to confirm a friend's subscription and apply your free months. You can delete this in Gainline → Profile → Delete My Referral Data.

Subscriptions

Payments are handled by Apple. We don't see your payment details.

No tracking

Gainline has no ads, no third-party analytics and doesn't track you across apps or websites.
