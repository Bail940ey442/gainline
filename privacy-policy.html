Gainline Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 November 2026.

Health and workout data stays on your devices

Gainline reads and writes workouts, heart rate, calories, swimming distance and strokes, VO₂ max, resting heart rate, heart rate variability and heart rate recovery through Apple Health, only with your permission. Your workouts, sets, routines, food log and settings are stored on your iPhone (encrypted while it's locked), shared with your own Apple Watch, and backed up to your private iCloud account so they survive reinstalling the app or moving to a new phone. Data in your private iCloud is only accessible to you; we can't see it. Gainline never sends health or workout data to us, and only sends it to Strava if you connect it, never uses it for advertising, and never sells it.

Meal estimates with Claude (optional)

If you add your own Anthropic API key, meals you describe in Log Food are sent directly from your iPhone to Anthropic so Claude can estimate their calories. Only the text you type is sent, never your health data, and it doesn't pass through our servers. Your key is kept in your iPhone's Keychain and usage is billed to your Anthropic account. Anthropic's privacy policy covers data sent to them. Remove the key any time in Profile → Integrations → Claude.

Strava (optional)

If you connect Strava, Gainline sends the workouts you choose (or, if you turn it on, each new workout) to your Strava account: title, time, duration, heart rate, calories, distance, and your sets, laps or steps. This only happens after you connect and agree. Data on Strava is covered by Strava's privacy policy. Your Strava sign-in tokens are kept in your iPhone's Keychain; our server only exchanges them with Strava and doesn't store them. Disconnect any time in Profile.

Sign in with Apple (optional)

Your name and email stay on your iPhone. Our server only receives a one-way hash of your Apple user ID, to keep your referral code with your account. Deleting your account in Profile removes this and revokes Gainline's access to your Apple ID.

Referrals

If you use referrals, our server stores a random account identifier from the App Store (not your name, email or Apple ID), your referral code, and the App Store transaction IDs needed to confirm a friend's subscription and apply your free months. You can delete this in Gainline → Profile → Delete My Referral Data.

Subscriptions

Payments are handled by Apple. We don't see your payment details.

No tracking

Gainline has no ads, no third-party analytics and doesn't track you across apps or websites.
